# Shop floor on your phone (Manufacturing pack)

For operators, inspectors and technicians whose plant uses the Manufacturing pack. Your plant may have changed the words
and colours; the steps are the same.

## Before the first shift

1. Your supervisor gives you the address and your sign-in. Open it on the phone, sign in.
2. Open **My apps** and add your app to the home screen (Android: menu > Add to Home screen; iPhone: Share > Add to
   Home Screen). Operators use **Shop floor**, inspectors **Inspection**, technicians **Maintenance**.
3. Open the app once while you have Wi-Fi or mobile data. After that it keeps working when the connection drops.

## Log production (operators)

1. Open **Shop floor**. Tap the camera button next to **Scan the machine QR code** and point it at the label on the
   machine. (A handheld scanner works too; or type the code and press Enter.) The machine's name appears.
2. Tap **Log production**. Machine, shift and time are filled in.
3. Type **Good quantity** and **Rejected quantity** (every piece that was not good the first time, scrapped or
   reworked), and how many of the rejected were **reworked**. **Run minutes** starts at the shift's planned minutes:
   subtract the time the machine stood still.
4. Add a **photo** if something is worth showing, then **Save**.

## Record a stoppage

Scan the machine, tap **Record a stoppage**, choose the **Reason** (and the detail), save. Leave **Ended on** empty while
the machine still stands; fill it in when it runs again. A **Breakdown** sends a maintenance request to the technician
at once.

## No connection?

You can still save. The bottom of the screen says **You're offline** and **1 waiting to send** (a photo counts as one
more). Keep the app open; when the connection returns everything is sent once, in order. Do not save the same entry
twice. If a saved entry is refused (for example a machine code that does not exist), the waiting list shows why: fix it
and send again, or discard it.

## Inspectors

**Inspection** > scan the lot label > **+ New inspection**. Choose the plan, type the measured value (a value outside the
limits is saved as Fail by itself), take a photo, sign with your finger, **Save**. Then add each defect (type,
quantity, action). A failed inspection raises an NCR for the quality team.

## Technicians

**Maintenance** > **Requests** shows the open requests for you. Open one, tap **Acknowledge** as soon as you take it
(after 30 minutes without it the plant manager is told). To close: set **Status** to Closed, write the **cause** and the
**action taken**, sign, **Save**. **PM tasks** lists the preventive maintenance due this week: open, do the checklist,
set **Done**, sign, save. **Report** raises a request yourself (scan the machine first).

## If something goes wrong

Take a screenshot with the message, note the time and the machine, and tell your supervisor. See
[When something goes wrong](something-wrong.md).
