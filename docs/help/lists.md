# Searching, sorting and filtering lists

Lists of records (a table of orders, a list of machines) are built by your company, so the exact buttons differ. These are the
things that work in the standard table and list screens. If your app has its own filter boxes or drop-downs, use them as they are.

## Search

If the table has a search box (it says **Search**), type a word. The list narrows to rows that contain it. If nothing matches the list
says **No matching rows**. Clear the box to see everything again. Some apps have a separate search box above the list: type and
press Enter, or just type.

## Sort

Click a **column heading** to sort by it. Click again to reverse the order (ascending or descending). Not every table lets you sort.

## Pages

Long lists show part of the rows at a time. Use the page buttons at the bottom (first, previous, next, last page). Some lists load more
as you scroll down.

## Filtering

Filtering depends on the app. Your company may give you a drop-down (for example "Status"), buttons, or a search box that narrows the list. If you
need a filter that does not exist, ask your administrator or the person who builds your apps: they can add one.

## "You only see some rows"

You see only the rows your role allows: for example only your own entries, or only your team's. A colleague may see different rows in the
same app. This is normal and is set by your administrator. If you think you should see a row that you cannot, tell your administrator the
record's name or number, and what you expected.

## Selecting rows

Click a row to select it. Tables that allow it have check boxes to select several rows. **Select all** selects the rows on this page.

## Numbers and totals

Totals and counts cover the rows **you** are allowed to see, so your total may differ from a colleague's.

## If the list is empty or slow

- Empty and you did not expect it: check the search box and any filter, then see [When something goes wrong](something-wrong.md).
- A message such as "This query took longer than 15 seconds and was stopped": tell your administrator which screen and the time.
- "Live updates paused" at the top: the list refreshes by itself every 30 seconds instead of at once. Everything still works.

## Export

Most operators cannot download a list unless the app has a button for it. If you need your data in Excel, ask your administrator:
a report that arrives by email may be the answer ([Reports you receive](scheduled-emails.md)).
