# Tasks

**Tasks** (left menu, User mode) lists what you have to do: tasks assigned to you or your teams, and your own.

## Add and finish a task

Type in **Add a task**, choose a due date if you like (on a phone this opens your device's date picker), and press **Add**.
Press the circle at the left of a task to mark it **Done**; press it again to reopen it. The **Board** view shows Open, In
progress and Done side by side (one at a time on a phone).

## Open a task

Press a task to open its details: description, priority, due date and time, **Remind me** (you get a notification at that time),
who it is assigned to, and comments.

## Repeat a task

Turn on **Repeat this task**, choose for example *every week on Monday*, and save. When you mark the task Done, the next one is
created for you. Choose **until** to stop the series on a day.

## Share a task

If you own a task you can **Share with a person**. They can read it and its comments. Others cannot see it. Remove the person
with the **x** to stop sharing. Use the **Which tasks** list to see tasks you assigned to others, or tasks shared with you.

## Reminders and the morning summary

You get one summary notification a day, after 08:00 your time, when you have overdue tasks or tasks due today. Tasks belong to
the environment you are in.
