# Viewing dashboards

Open **Dashboards** in the menu and pick a dashboard. You see the dashboards shared with your role.

- **Your numbers.** Each tile counts only the rows you are allowed to see. When your access is limited, the tile says "Based on the rows you
  can see". A colleague may see different totals on the same dashboard.
- **Date range.** Choose a range at the top (this shift, today, this week, this month, this fiscal year, fiscal year to date, ...). The dates
  it covers are shown next to it. "Compared with the previous period" uses the same part of the previous period: on the 19th, this month so
  far is compared with last month up to the 19th.
- **Filters.** A filter at the top (for example Shift) applies to every tile that has that column.
- **Click to filter.** On tiles that allow it (for example Output by machine), click a row or a bar: the other tiles show only that
  machine. Click the chip at the top to remove the filter.
- **Open an app.** Some tiles open an app when you click a point or a row (the app may start with that value already chosen), or have
  an **Open** button in their title bar. If you may not use that app, a message says so.
- **Details.** Point at a bar or a point to see its value. With the keyboard: Tab to a chart, then the arrow keys move between points.
- **As a table.** The table button of a tile shows its numbers as a table. The download button saves a PNG or SVG picture or a CSV file.
- **Up to date.** Tiles refresh by themselves when the data changes, and on the dashboard's refresh interval; the time of the last update
  is at the top right.
- **On a tablet** the tiles are shown in six columns, on a **phone** one below the other.
- **Pin a tile.** The pin icon in a tile's title bar puts it on **My dashboard** at the top of **My apps**. There you see it with your own
  numbers; the arrows change the order, the cross unpins it, and the dashboard's name opens the whole dashboard. A pin whose dashboard or
  tile was removed, or that you can no longer open, says "No longer available": choose **Remove**.
- **By email.** **Email** at the top sends the dashboard to you as a PDF on a schedule (**New snapshot**), or once (**Send me a copy** on a
  snapshot you receive). The PDF has the numbers your own access allows. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
- **On a TV.** A screen that shows a dashboard without anyone signed in was set up by the dashboard's owner or an administrator
  (**Screens**). If it says "This screen's view token does not work", ask them for a new address.
- **Settings.** **Settings** on the dashboard list shows the time zone, fiscal year, week start, shifts, locale and currency that the
  date ranges and numbers follow. Only administrators can change them.

If a tile shows a message instead of a chart (for example "You do not have access to the column ..."), the other tiles still work: ask the
person who made the dashboard.
