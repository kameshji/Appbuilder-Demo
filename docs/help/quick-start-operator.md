# Quick-start for operators (one page, print me)

**Your administrator:** ______________________  **Phone / WhatsApp:** ______________________
**Address of Appbuilder:** ______________________________________

## 1. Sign in
1. Open the address above. Type **Email** and **Password**, press **Sign in**.
2. If asked, type the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
3. Forgot the password? **Forgot your password?** Still stuck: ask your administrator for a reset code.
4. Lost your phone? Use **Use a recovery code**, or ask your administrator to reset your two-step verification.

## 2. Open your app
**My apps** > find your app (**Search your apps...**) > **Open**. It opens as a tab. Close it with its x.
No apps? Ask your administrator to add the app to your role.

## 3. Enter data
- Press the new-record button, fill the fields, press **Save**. Wait for "Saving..." to finish.
- Red text under a field tells you what to fix. **Fix the highlighted fields**, then save again.
- A lock or **Read-only for your role** means you cannot change that field.

## 4. Find a record
Type in **Search**. Click a column heading to sort. Use the page buttons at the bottom. You see only the rows your role allows.

## 5. Talk about a record
Write in the comment box. Type **@** and a name to mention a colleague. **Task** adds a to-do. **Follow** tells you about new comments.

## 6. "This record was changed by ..."
Someone saved the same record. **Do not rush.** **Reload** shows their version and drops what you typed. **Keep mine** replaces theirs with yours. When unsure, ask the person named.

## 7. Notifications
The **bell** at the top lists what needs you. Settings > Notifications: choose **Right away**, **In a digest** or **No email**.

## 8. Something went wrong
1. Stop. Do not repeat it many times.
2. Take a screenshot with the message visible.
3. Write down the time, the app and the record.
4. Tell your administrator (what you did, the exact message, the time).

## Never
- Never share your password, phone code or recovery codes.
- Never use someone else's account.
- Do not close an error message before you have read it and taken a screenshot.

Full help: see the Help index (ask your administrator where it is kept).
