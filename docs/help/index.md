# Help for people who use Appbuilder at work

This help is for operators, supervisors and office staff. You do not need to know anything technical. Your company
builds its own apps, so the screens you work in may look different from the pictures here. The buttons and messages that
every app shares (signing in, notifications, comments, tasks, the message "This record was changed by ...") are the same
everywhere, and that is what these pages describe.

"Appbuilder" is a working name; your company may call it something else.

## Who to ask

Your **administrator** is the person in your company who looks after Appbuilder (adds people, gives access, fixes
problems). Ask them first. If you do not know who it is, ask your supervisor. Please do not contact the people who made
Appbuilder directly unless your administrator tells you to.

## Three rules that keep everyone safe

1. Never share your password, the code from your phone app, or your recovery codes. Nobody from the support team will ever ask
   for them.
2. Use your own account. Every change is recorded under the name that made it.
3. When you see an error, do not close it. Read it, take a screenshot, and tell your administrator ([When something goes
   wrong](something-wrong.md)).

## What is not available yet

Some things people ask about are not built yet, for example approvals inbox screens, dashboards, chat, WhatsApp
messages, a Hindi screen language and printing as PDF. Entries marked **Soon** in the left menu are planned; they open a short note
and do nothing else. If something you need is missing, tell your administrator.
