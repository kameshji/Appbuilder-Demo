# Printing and PDFs

Many apps have a **Print** button, or print from a menu: the document (an inspection report, a challan, a purchase
request) is made as a PDF on the server and opens in a new browser tab, where you can print it, save it or share it.

- **On a computer** the PDF opens in a new tab. If nothing opens, your browser blocked the tab: allow pop-ups for
  Appbuilder (the icon at the right of the address bar).
- **On a phone**, once the PDF is ready a bar at the bottom offers **Share** (send it to WhatsApp, email or any app
  on the phone) and **Download**. Phones whose browser cannot share files download the PDF straight away; open it from
  the download notice to view, print or share it.

## What the document shows

The document shows what **you** are allowed to see. A colleague with more access may get a fuller document of the same
record; if you cannot open the record at all, you get "The record was not found, or you may not read it".

## Documents stored with a record

In a record's timeline, **Document** lists the print templates of the record's table; pick one and the PDF is made and
kept in the record's documents, so others who can read the record find it there later.

## Documents by email

A flow or a scheduled report can send you a PDF every day (for example the production summary). Each email's PDF is made
with your own access. See [scheduled emails](scheduled-emails.md).

## Hindi

Hindi text prints correctly and can be copied and searched in the PDF.
