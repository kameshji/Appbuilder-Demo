# Sales on the go (CRM pack)

For sales reps whose company uses the CRM pack. Your company may have changed the words; the steps are the same.

## Before the first day

1. Your manager gives you the address and your sign-in. Open it on the phone and sign in.
2. Open **My apps** and add **Sales on the go** to the home screen (Android: menu > Add to Home screen; iPhone: Share >
   Add to Home Screen).

## See your deals

**My deals** lists the open deals that are yours, soonest closing date first; type in the search box to find one. You see
only your own deals. **Accounts** lists your customers: tap one to see its phone, email, deals and people.

## Update a deal

1. Tap the deal. Its stage, value and closing date are at the top.
2. Change the **Stage** (the win chance follows it), the **Amount**, the **Close date** or the **Next step**, then
   **Save**. When you set a deal to **Lost**, choose the **Loss reason**.
3. The **Timeline** at the bottom shows the calls, meetings, emails and messages on this deal.

## Log a call or a meeting

1. Open the deal and tap **Log a call** or **Log a meeting**.
2. Write a short subject (what was said or agreed) and, if you like, notes. Tap **Save**.
3. It appears on the deal's timeline straight away, for you and for your manager. You need a connection to save it.

## If something is wrong

A deal you cannot find may belong to a colleague: you see only your own. A message under a button in red says what to
fix. Anything else: [something is wrong](something-wrong.md).
