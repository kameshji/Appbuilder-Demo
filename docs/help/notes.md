# Notes

**Notes** (left menu, User mode) keeps your notes: meeting notes, visit reports, checklists, anything you want to write
down. A note is private until you share it. Notes belong to the environment you are in.

## Write a note

Press **New**. Give the note a title and write. The bar above the text has bold, italic, underline, three heading sizes,
quotes, bulleted and numbered lists, **checklists** (press the box to tick an item), links, **tables** (with buttons to add a
row or a column while you are in a cell), images and a link to a record. Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+U and Ctrl+K (link) work too.
You can type in any language your keyboard supports, Hindi included.

There is no Save button: the note is saved a moment after you stop typing. The top of the note says **Saving...**,
**Saved** or **Unsaved changes**. Text you paste from a web page or a document keeps its headings, lists, links and tables;
colours, fonts and anything else are left out.

## Mention someone

Type **@** and a few letters of a name, then choose the person. They get a notification with a link to the note. Only
people who can open the note can be mentioned: if they cannot, and the note is yours, you are asked whether to share it
with them first (they can then read it).

## Link a record

Press the record button, choose a table and a record. The note shows a small card with the record's name. Everyone sees
the card as they are allowed to: someone who cannot open that record sees "Record not available", never its name.

## Images and files

Press the image button (or paste an image) to put a picture in the text. **Attach a file** at the bottom adds any other
file. Pictures and files belong to the note: whoever can open the note can see them.

## Share a note

Press **Share** (only the owner of a note can). Find a person by name or email, or choose a team, and choose **Can read**
or **Can edit**. People with "Can edit" can change the text, but only you can share the note or delete it. Members of a
team see the note while they are in the team.

## Notes about a record

A note can be about a record, for example an order. Everyone who can open that order sees the note on the order's
history (its timeline), without sharing it. Start one with **New note about a record...** on the Notes page, or with
**Note** on the record's timeline.

## Find a note

Search looks at the titles and the text of every note you can open; the words you searched for are marked. The views:
**Recent** (the last changed first), **Pinned** (press the pin on a note), **Shared with me**, **About records** (grouped by
record) and **Mine**.

## When someone else changed the note

If the same note was changed in another tab or by someone with "Can edit" while you were typing, nothing is overwritten:
you are asked which text to keep, **Keep mine**, **Take the saved text**, or, when you changed different paragraphs,
**Merge both**. A note you are only reading updates by itself.

## Quick note

The note button at the top of every page opens a small box: write, press **Save**, and the note is in Notes.

## Print

The printer button prints only the note; choose "Save as PDF" in the print window for a PDF file.

## On a phone and offline

On a phone the list and the note each take the whole screen; the arrow goes back to the list. Notes need a connection:
offline, the page says so and the note cannot be changed until the connection is back. Notes are not kept on the phone.
