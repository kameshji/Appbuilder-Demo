## The blunt picture

- **No customer, no pilot, no outside security review, v0.5.** The first three deals are bought on you, not on the
  product. Sell your process skill and a short, paid, bounded pilot.
- **What sells today is office-side.** Data, security, validation, formula columns, scheduled email reports, backups.
  The shop-floor features buyers ask for first (phone entry, approvals, WhatsApp, PDF) are in progress or planned. The
  simulations show those gaps touching 7 of 8 conversations.
- **There is a tension to decide.** The [master plan](../../plan/master-plan.md#5-build-phases) says the pilot starts
  after P3 (flows, phone, PDF, WhatsApp, templates). Selling earlier means office-side pilots only, and every hour
  selling is an hour not building P1/P2. Recommendation: in the first 90 days after notice end, aim for **2 paid
  office-side pilots** (3 is the stretch goal), and protect three mornings a week for product.
- **Prove the Windows installer** on two real Windows PCs before any customer sees it (README: not yet run on Windows).

## The employment constraint

BRIEF: the founder is serving notice at an Indian IT services company with a no-moonlighting policy until **about end
of January 2027**. Until then, and until a lawyer has read the employment contract and confirmed what is allowed:

**Treat everything as learning and discovery only.** No quotes, prices, proposals, pilots, demos framed as offers,
invoices, signed agreements, payment, or promises of future delivery. No use of the employer's time, devices, contacts,
clients or confidential information. Do not approach the employer's clients.

**Flag for the lawyer (questions to bring):**
1. Does the contract's no-moonlighting or conflict clause cover unpaid customer research interviews about my own
   product idea? Building the product in my own time? Forming an entity or registering GST before the last day?
2. Are there post-employment restrictions (non-compete, non-solicit of clients or staff, IP assignment of work done
   during employment) that affect this product or its sales after the notice period?
3. Can a partner or family member form the entity before my last day, and what may I do for it?
4. What wording should the research-interview request use?

Until the answer comes back, even the **[Research]** templates in the outreach kit should wait.

## 30-60-90 after notice end (day 1 = first day selling is confirmed allowed)

Targets assume the **base case** in [pipeline maths](sales-playbook.md#15-pipeline-maths-for-a-solo-founder);
re-plan at day 30 with real conversion rates.

### Days 1-30: fill the funnel
- Activate introducers: 5 CA/Tally-partner meetings; 2 signed referral agreements.
- 25 discovery conversations (≈ 6/week), 8 plant walks, 4 demos on customers' own sheets.
- 2 pilot proposals out by day 30.
- Every conversation logged with "Product gaps raised".
- Product: 3 mornings/week; nothing else may take them.
- **Exit check:** ≥ 20 conversations held; ≥ 2 proposals out; installer proven on a customer-like PC.

### Days 31-60: first pilots live
- Sign and install pilot 1 and pilot 2 (≤ 1 day each); weekly reviews every Thursday.
- Continue 6-8 conversations/week; 4 more demos; 2-3 more proposals.
- First win/loss review (use [simulations.md](simulations.md) format on real deals).
- **Exit check:** 2 paid pilots running; no pilot below 50% of its usage criterion at week 2 without a recovery plan
  (see simulation h).

### Days 61-90: convert and learn
- Pilot 1 week-6 decision → licence and rollout (or honest stop with export).
- Sign pilot 3 (stretch goal).
- First referral asks to happy pilot owners; first partner-sourced demo.
- Update the playbook: real conversion rates, real objection frequencies, real gap ranking. Feed the gap ranking into
  the P1/P2 priorities.
- **Exit check:** ≥ 1 licence converted or a written reason why not; ≥ 2 pilots run to decision; ROI baseline vs
  actual measured on at least one pilot.

### Scorecard (review every Monday)

| Metric | Day 30 | Day 60 | Day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery conversations (cumulative) | 25 | 50 | 70 |
| Plant walks | 8 | 16 | 22 |
| Demos on customer data | 4 | 9 | 13 |
| Proposals | 2 | 5 | 7 |
| Paid pilots signed | 0-1 | 2 | 2-3 |
| Licences converted | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| Product mornings kept (of 3/week) | 12 | 24 | 36 |

All targets are **Assumptions**: they come from the base-case conversion rates, not from data.
