(version 0.5.0). Nothing here is legal or tax advice. Product facts come only from [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) and the
product docs.

## The plan on one line per stage

0. **Pilot readiness** (to about Mar 2027): finish a pilot-minimum product (flows, approvals, notifications, three
   phone entry screens, one OEE board, three manufacturing apps, proven Windows install); no commercial activity
   until the last working day; incorporate the day after.
1. **One design partner** (about Mar-Jun 2027) in Govindpura (Bhopal) or Mandideep; free 90-day pilot with a
   pre-agreed conversion price.
2. **Three to five paid pilots** (about Jul-Dec 2027), extending to Indore and Pithampur; outside security review.
3. **Public launch in India** (2028 H1 earliest).
4. **Global** (not before 2028 H2).
