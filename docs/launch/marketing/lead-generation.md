## 1. Compliance first (what the rules say, and what we do)

| Rule | What it says (sourced) | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| **DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025** | The Act does not apply to personal data the person made public themselves, or that someone was legally required to publish; the test is how the data became public, not whether it can be found online, and collection may still raise other legal issues ([India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/india-dpdp-act-publicly-available-personal-data-46899.html/)). The "legitimate uses" in section 7 do not include marketing or a general legitimate-interest basis ([DPDPA section 7](https://www.dpdpa.com/dpdpa2023/chapter-2/section7.html)). Most obligations (notice, consent, security, breach notice) apply from about 13 May 2027 ([manufacturing.md §5](../../research/manufacturing.md)). | Company facts (name, address, GSTIN, products) are not personal data: collect freely from lawful public sources. For a named person's email or mobile: use it only if that person published it for business contact (own website, own LinkedIn profile, own listing) and record where it came from, or get consent. Everything else waits for an opt-in. Keep a provenance column for every contact. |
| **TRAI TCCCPR (2025 amendment)** | Promotional calls must come from the 140 series; transactional/service from the new 1600 series; senders may not use normal 10-digit numbers for telemarketing; 5 complaints in 10 days triggers action; first violation bars the sender's outgoing services for 15 days, later violations mean one-year disconnection and blacklisting; promotional messages need an opt-out ([TRAI press release 11/2025](https://cms.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/PR_No.11of2025.pdf)). | **No cold promotional calling or SMS from the founder's mobile.** Phone is used only when the prospect asked for a call or is already in a conversation with us. Any volume calling or SMS later goes through a DLT-registered provider on the proper series. Whether a one-to-one call to a business landline counts as telemarketing is **Unverified**: ask the lawyer. |
| **Draft CCPA guidelines on unsolicited commercial communication (2026)** | A draft would extend consent rules beyond telecom to OTT apps (such as WhatsApp), RCS and social media; it names adding consumers to promotional groups without consent and persistent follow-ups after a single enquiry as unsolicited; it proposes a grievance officer and keeping consent records. Status: draft, several provisions marked for revision ([Khaitan & Co summary](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2026-10/Ergo_CCPA%20Guidelines_6%20October%202026.pdf)). Whether it covers B2B messages to business owners is **Unverified**. | Act as if it applies: max 3 follow-ups per sequence; never add anyone to a group; keep a consent log (who, when, how, for what); name a grievance contact on the website. |
| **WhatsApp Business messaging policy** | Contact only people whose number you have and who opted in; the opt-in must clearly say they are opting in to messages from the named business; give clear opt-out instructions and honour them ([Meta: Get opt-in for WhatsApp](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in)). | WhatsApp is never a cold channel. Opt-in via website form, QR at events, or a click-to-chat link the prospect taps. Opt-in text: "Yes, send me updates from `<NAME>` on WhatsApp. Reply STOP anytime." |
| **IndiaMART terms** | Prohibit "systematic retrieval" of IndiaMART content "to create or compile" a database, "whether through robots, spiders, automatic devices or manual processes", and spamming ([IndiaMART terms of use](https://m.indiamart.com/terms-of-use.html)). | Do **not** build lists from IndiaMART, manually or automatically. Use it only to read about a company already on our list. Never scrape, never buy "IndiaMART data". |
| **GST public search** | Search is by GSTIN or PAN; finding a GSTIN by business name is not supported ([Quicko](https://learn.quicko.com/gst-portal-taxpayer-details)). | Use it to verify a company's legal name and status, not to discover companies. |
| **LinkedIn** | LinkedIn's User Agreement prohibits scraping and automation tools (**Unverified** in this research; widely known; check current text). | Manual research and manual messages only. No automation extensions. |
| **Email** | No India-specific anti-spam statute was found in this research (**Unverified**). UK (Phase D): unsolicited marketing email to corporate subscribers is allowed without consent, but not to sole traders or partnerships, and the sender must not hide identity and must give an opt-out address ([ICO](https://cy.ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-direct-marketing-using-electronic-mail/how-do-we-comply-with-the-pecr-electronic-mail-marketing-rules/)). | Every email: real name, company, postal address, one-line reason for writing, an opt-out line honoured within 24 hours. Send from a separate domain/subdomain once the name exists; never more than ~20 cold emails/day (**Assumption**, deliverability). |

Also: never buy contact databases ("10,000 manufacturers of MP with mobile numbers"). Provenance cannot be shown, and
the DPDP and TRAI risk lands on us.

## 4. Outbound sequences

Rules for all sequences: max 4 touches over ~3 weeks then stop; every message has a reason specific to the company;
no attachments in the first email; no claims beyond BRIEF; planned features are named as planned. Stop on any
"not interested" and record it.

## 6. Clinics, webinars and demo days

**"Excel-se-App Clinic" (in person, 2 hours, association hall, Hindi-English).**
- 0:00 Tea, sign-in sheet with separate, unticked consent boxes (email updates / WhatsApp updates / photo use).
- 0:15 Talk (20 min): "7 registers every plant has, and what goes wrong with each" (no product).
- 0:35 Live build (30 min): an attendee volunteers a register; build it live on a laptop with sample data; show roles
  and "test as user".
- 1:05 Honest roadmap (5 min): what's built, in progress and planned.
- 1:10 One-to-one corner (40 min): scorecard (magnet 4) filled in with each owner.
- 1:50 Close: design-partner places; QR to WhatsApp opt-in.
Cost: hall (often free for members, **Unverified**), tea, 30 printed scorecards. Target 10-20 attendees.

**Online webinar (45 min, monthly from Phase B):** "Build a breakdown log in 30 minutes" live; Q&A; recording on
YouTube. Registration form with consent boxes.

**Demo day (Phase C):** at the pilot customer's plant if they agree, for 5-8 owners from the same cluster.

## 8. Qualification framework and lead scoring

Framework "FPAT": **Fit** (company), **Pain** (specific, felt), **Access** (decision-maker reachable), **Timing** (trigger).

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Fit: manufacturer 20-200 staff (ICP 1) | 20 |
| Fit: services firm 20-50 or PP consultant with clients (ICP 2/3) | 15 |
| Fit: has a PC/server on site and someone Excel-literate | 10 |
| Pain: names a register or report that hurts (breakdowns, QC, approvals, daily report) | 20 |
| Pain: has tried and dropped an ERP or app | 5 |
| Access: owner or plant head in the conversation | 15 |
| Timing: audit, new line, expansion, staff exit within 3 months | 15 |
| Engagement: attended clinic/webinar or downloaded 2+ magnets | 5 |
| Negative: needs phone-only use, WhatsApp alerts or PDF on day one (not built) | -20 (record as "nurture until P2") |
| Negative: wants full ERP/accounting replacement | -15 |

60+ = qualified (book discovery). 40-59 = nurture (newsletter, invite to next clinic). Under 40 = no action.

Discovery call checklist (30 min): current registers; who enters, who reads; devices on the floor; Tally/ERP version;
server PC available; internet quality; what "good" looks like in 90 days; who signs; what would make this fail.

## 10. Weekly cadence for a solo founder

The founder also builds the product. Marketing time must be capped and protected.

**Phase A (about 10 hours/week):**

| Day | Block | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Research 10 accounts + score signals | 1.5 |
| Tue | Write and schedule 3 LinkedIn posts | 1.5 |
| Wed | Outbound: 10 personalised first touches + follow-ups due | 1.5 |
| Thu | Record and edit 1 Hindi-English video (phone + free editor) | 2 |
| Fri | Calls / visits (discovery) | 2 |
| Sat (alternate) | Clinic or association meeting | 1 (averaged) |
| Sun | 30-min review: metrics, pipeline, next week | 0.5 |

**Phase B (about 8 hours/week marketing + pilot time):** pilot visit weekly (not counted), 2 posts/week from pilot
learnings, case-study drafting, partner calls.

**Phase C (about 20 hours/week, or hire):** add ads management (1 h), partner enablement (3 h), two clinics/month,
demos (5-8 h). This is the point to hire a sales/partner associate (budget tier ₹5 lakh in [strategy.md §7](strategy.md)).

Weekly targets in Phase A (**Assumption**): 10 accounts researched, 10 first touches, 3 conversations, 1 video,
3 posts, 0 cold calls.
